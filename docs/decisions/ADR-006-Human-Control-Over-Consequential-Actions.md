# ADR 006: Human Control Over Consequential Actions

## Status
Draft

## Context
The system evaluates risk and recommends decisions, but autonomous actions (like merging or deploying) carry high risk.

## Decision
Humans retain control over consequential actions. The system will recommend actions, but a human must confirm or trigger them.

## Consequences
- Reduces risk of catastrophic automated failures.
- Builds trust with users.
